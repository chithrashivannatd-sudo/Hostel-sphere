Hostel Sphere Features

An all-in-one residential management platform engineered for colleges and universities.

🛏️ Automated Room & Bed Allocation

Real-time gender segregation, capacity checks, and bed availability tracking across all floors.

💳 Instant Fees & Official Receipts

Installment tracking, payment history, and downloadable PDF-ready fee statements with student seals.

🚪 24/7 Digital Gate Passes & Visitor Logs

Automated curfew tracking, warden approvals for late entries, and student visitor request workflows.

🛠️ Maintenance & Complaints Ticketing

Upload photos with complaints and track real-time resolution by the campus maintenance crew.
